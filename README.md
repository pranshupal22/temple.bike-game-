# Street Sprint — Bike Game

A small browser bike game. Avoid road hazards, jump over obstacles, and collect stars to increase your score.

## How to play

1. Open `bike-game.html` in a web browser.
2. Select **Start Riding**.
3. Steer between lanes and jump over hazards.

### Controls

- **Left / Right arrow** or **A / D**: steer
- **Space** or **Up arrow**: jump
- On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen buttons

Your best score is saved in the browser on your device.
